## 二、計算題詳細過程與解析（每題 4 分，共 44 分）

1. **$(-2 - 3) - (-5 - 8)$**
   $$\text{原式} = (-5) - (-13) = -5 + 13 = \mathbf{8}$$

2. **$267 - 456 - (-33) + 156$**
   $$\text{原式} = 267 - 456 + 33 + 156 = (267 + 33 + 156) - 456 = 456 - 456 = \mathbf{0}$$

3. **$(-36) + 49 - (-15) - 61$**
   $$\text{原式} = -36 + 49 + 15 - 61 = (49 + 15) - (36 + 61) = 64 - 97 = \mathbf{-33}$$

4. **$-15 - 95 - 995 - 9995$**
   $$\begin{aligned}
   \text{原式} &= -(15 + 95 + 995 + 9995) \\
   &= -[(20 - 5) + (100 - 5) + (1000 - 5) + (10000 - 5)] \\
   &= -(11120 - 20) = \mathbf{-11100}
   \end{aligned}$$

5. **$(-6) \times 7 + (-8) \times (-5)$**
   $$\text{原式} = (-42) + (+40) = \mathbf{-2}$$

6. **$4 \times (-15) - (-21) \div 3$**
   $$\text{原式} = (-60) - (-7) = -60 + 7 = \mathbf{-53}$$

7. **$(-7) \times (-4) + (12 - 18) \div 3$**
   $$\text{原式} = (+28) + (-6) \div 3 = 28 + (-2) = \mathbf{26}$$

8. **$(-12) + 3 \times [(-5) - (-3)]$**
   $$\text{原式} = (-12) + 3 \times [-5 + 3] = -12 + 3 \times (-2) = -12 - 6 = \mathbf{-18}$$

9. **$[24 - 2 \times (-3)] \div 3 - (-3)$**
   $$\text{原式} = [24 - (-6)] \div 3 + 3 = [24 + 6] \div 3 + 3 = 30 \div 3 + 3 = 10 + 3 = \mathbf{13}$$

10. **$26 \div [28 \div 7 - (-3) \times 3] + 7$**
    $$\text{原式} = 26 \div [4 - (-9)] + 7 = 26 \div [4 + 9] + 7 = 26 \div 13 + 7 = 2 + 7 = \mathbf{9}$$

11. **$9 + (-2) \times [18 - (-5) \times 3] \div 3$**
    $$\begin{aligned}
    \text{原式} &= 9 + (-2) \times [18 - (-15)] \div 3 \\
    &= 9 + (-2) \times [18 + 15] \div 3 \\
    &= 9 + (-2) \times 33 \div 3 \\
    &= 9 + (-2) \times 11 = 9 - 22 = \mathbf{-13}
    \end{aligned}$$
